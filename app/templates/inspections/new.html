How it works

  1. You upload

    A clip or photos from your phone, dashcam or drone.

  2. OpenCV looks

    Finds the drain, water and debris, and checks what persists over time.

  3. AI rates severity

    A vision model double-checks the evidence and scores the risk.

  4. Agent recommends

    Compares history and rainfall, then drafts the next action.

  5. A person decides

    A supervisor approves, edits or rejects it.

Tips for good footage

  • Keep the drain grate in view
  • Hold steady for 10–20 seconds
  • Daylight works best
  • Faces and plates are blurred in reports
